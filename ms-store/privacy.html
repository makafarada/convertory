Convertory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Convertory ("the app") is a file conversion utility. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

What we collect

Convertory does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the app. We do not collect names, email addresses, contact lists, or location data.

File processing

When you convert a file, its contents are processed in order to produce the converted output. Files are used only to perform the conversion you request and are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Converted files are returned to you and are not retained by us for any purpose other than completing your request.

Advertising and tracking

Convertory does not display advertising and does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs that build an advertising profile of you.

Purchases

If the app offers an optional paid upgrade in the future, any payment is processed by the app store (Google Play, Microsoft Store, or Apple App Store) — Convertory itself does not collect or store your payment card details.

Children's privacy

Convertory is a general-purpose productivity utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact
